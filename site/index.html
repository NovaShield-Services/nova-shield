The signature service

Fitted once.
Changed whenever
you like.

A low-profile aluminium channel is fitted into the soffit and matched to its colour, so by day you see a trim line rather than a string of bulbs. Warm white on an ordinary Tuesday. Any colour you want on the evenings that are not ordinary.

  • Warm white as the default Most nights it simply reads as architectural lighting, because that is what it is.
  • Colour when the moment calls for it Holidays, birthdays, a team playing — set a scene from your phone, change it back the next morning.
  • Designed to disappear in daylight The channel is colour-matched to the soffit it sits in, so the hardware is not the feature.
