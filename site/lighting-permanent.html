The difference it makes

Most houses
disappear at dusk.

A home you have spent years on becomes a dark shape for half the year. Northern Ontario evenings start early, and the architecture you paid for — the gables, the porch line, the way the roof steps down over the garage — simply stops being visible.

Permanent lighting is not about brightness. It is about keeping the shape of the house readable after dark, in a way that looks deliberate rather than decorated.

The system

Fitted once.
Changed whenever
you like.

Each LED is individually addressable, so the same installation produces a restrained warm white on an ordinary evening and a full colour scene when you want one. You change it from your phone; nothing on the house changes.

  • Warm white as the default Most nights it simply reads as architectural lighting, because that is what it is.
  • Colour on the evenings that are not ordinary Set a scene, leave it for the night, put it back the next morning.
  • Scheduled, so you stop thinking about it On at dusk, off at a time you choose. No timer to reset when the clocks change.

Designed to disappear

By day, a trim line.
Not a string of bulbs.

The channel is aluminium, low-profile, and comes in colours matched to common soffit and fascia tones. Fitted up under the edge, it reads as part of the trim from the street.

We install the Permanent Lighting Direct platform. It is low-voltage, which is why the channel can be slim and why there is no mains cabling running around your roofline.
