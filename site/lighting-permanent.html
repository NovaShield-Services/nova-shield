What it actually is

Not holiday lights that stay up.

Individually controllable LED points sit inside a low-profile aluminium channel fixed under the soffit or fascia. The channel comes in colours matched to common soffit tones, so from the street during the day you see a trim line, not a string of bulbs.

  • Warm white as the default Most nights it just looks like tasteful architectural lighting, because that is what it is.
  • Colour on demand Holidays, birthdays, a team playing — set a scene from your phone and change it back the next morning.
  • Scheduled, so you forget about it On at dusk, off at a time you choose. No timers to reset when the clocks change.
  • Laid out for your roofline We measure the actual elevations. Peaks, dormers and garage returns are planned, not improvised on the day.

The system

Built for here, not a mild climate.

We install the Permanent Lighting Direct platform. It is a low-voltage system, which is why the channel can be slim and why there is no mains cabling running around your roofline.
