The arrangement

You are not buying
lights. You are
buying the season.

The display stays ours. That is the point of it — because everything that normally makes holiday lighting a chore stays ours as well.

  • No set to buy No upfront purchase, and no replacing a display that has faded to three different shades of white.
  • No ladder in November On a roofline, in the cold, in Northern Ontario. We would rather it was us.
  • Nothing stored in your garage It does not take up a shelf for ten months of the year.
  • A failure is ours to fix If something in your stored set stops working, it is repaired or replaced before it goes back up.
Fitted to your house

Cut once,
then reused.

The first season we measure your elevations and build the display to fit them — the runs are cut and laid out for your roofline specifically. After that, the same display goes back on the same house, which is why it fits and why it keeps fitting.

  • A cheaper second season The first year covers the design and the set. After that you are paying for installation and removal, not for building it again.
  • Commercial-grade and modular We use modular lighting specifically so a failed section can be replaced instead of the whole run.
  • Two visits, and nothing else One to install, one to remove. You do not need to be home for either.

Straight answers

The questions
people actually ask.

So I do not own the lights?

Correct — and that is deliberate. It is why you are not buying a set, not storing it, and not paying for it when a section fails. Your display is kept under your name and goes back on your house next season.

What if I stop after one year?

Then we collect the set at removal and that is the end of it. You are never left with equipment you did not want, or a contract for seasons you have not asked for.

Can I have it lit before December?

Yes. We install through October and November and you decide when it comes on. Plenty of people have it fitted early and leave it dark until they are ready.

Could I just have permanent lighting instead?

Plenty of people move to it after a season or two, because it covers the holidays and every other night of the year. Have a look at permanent lighting.
